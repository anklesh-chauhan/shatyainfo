# Shatya Info 30-Day Free Trial Terms

**Effective 2 November 2026 · Version 2026-11-02**

These Trial Terms supplement the Shatya Info Service Agreement. If they conflict about the free trial, these Trial Terms control for that subject.

1. **Start.** An eligible new company gets 30 consecutive days of free use. The clock begins when its workspace is ready after email verification and initial setup, not when the registration form is submitted. The Billing page displays the workspace-ready and trial-end dates.
2. **What is included.** All application modules are available during the trial. No card is needed to create a workspace. The company controls which users it invites. Normal technical limits needed to protect the service from misuse still apply.
3. **No charge during the trial.** There is no subscription fee for the first 30 days. The owner may cancel from Billing before the trial-end time and owe nothing for the trial. A cancellation confirmation should be retained by the Customer.
4. **After the trial.** Unless cancelled before the trial ends, the first paid month begins when the trial ends. The price is ₹59 per user licence per month, with at least 10 licences billed (₹590 per month). The bill uses the account count at the start of the paid month; mid-month changes can be sent to support for a prorated adjustment. These are the total displayed amounts. Shatya Info does not separately charge GST while it is not GST-registered and will notify Customers before any change in tax treatment. The company owner pays each upcoming month manually through Razorpay checkout. There is no automatic recurring card charge. An invoice created for a paid month remains due even if checkout is not completed.
5. **Reminders and cancellation.** We aim to email the registered owner during the final seven days of the trial; delivery depends on the address and mail system. The Customer can see the trial date in Billing without relying on email. After the trial, the owner may cancel any time; a paid month already begun and earlier unpaid bills remain payable, and no later monthly bills are created.
6. **Data at the end.** Cancellation does not immediately delete the workspace. After service ends, the Customer may email support to request a support-assisted export in CSV or Excel format within 30 days. Active copies are deleted within 7 days after a documented deletion request or the end of that window, and backup copies within a further 15 days, except for legally required retention.
7. **Eligibility and abuse.** The trial is intended for one genuine evaluation per company. We may refuse duplicate or abusive registrations after reasonable review, with notice where practicable.

The [Service Agreement](/legal/service-agreement) explains billing, cancellation, support, and overdue-payment notices in more detail.
