## 2. Trial and subscription

The [Trial Terms](/legal/trial-terms) form part of this Agreement. A new eligible workspace receives 30 consecutive days of free use starting when its initial setup is complete and the workspace is ready. No card is required to begin. The trial includes the full application. If the Customer cancels from Billing before the trial ends, no subscription fee is due for the trial.

If the Customer has not cancelled before the trial ends, the first paid month begins at the trial end time. The subscription price is **₹59 per user licence per month, with a minimum of 10 licences (₹590 per month)**. The automated bill uses the number of user accounts when the paid month begins, whether or not those accounts were used recently. If the Customer adds or disables an account during that month, it may request a prorated adjustment from support. We will calculate the adjustment for the days remaining in that paid month and apply it to the next bill or issue a credit. The ₹590 minimum for each paid month still applies. If its tax treatment changes, it will notify the Customer before a future billing period. Shatya Info creates an invoice for each upcoming paid month; the company owner pays it manually through Razorpay checkout. We do not automatically charge a card or bank account.

The Billing page shows invoices and payment status. An unpaid invoice remains payable even if payment has not been completed through checkout. We may contact the Customer about overdue invoices. We will not suspend access for non-payment without first giving the registered owner 30 days' written notice and an opportunity to pay. The notice will state the overdue amount, payment method, and proposed suspension date. If we suspend access, we will restore it after confirmed payment, subject to reasonable processing time.

## 3. Cancellation and end of service

The company owner may cancel at any time from Billing. Cancellation during the free trial incurs no subscription fee. After the trial, the paid month that has already begun remains payable, along with earlier unpaid invoices; no later monthly periods are billed. Cancellation does not immediately erase Customer Data. For 30 days after service ends, the Customer may email support to request a CSV or Excel export of its workspace data. This is a support-assisted export, not a single in-app download. We will delete or return active copies within 7 days after a documented deletion request or the end of the export window, and delete backup copies within a further 15 days, unless law requires retention. We will confirm completion on request.

Except for non-payment, which follows the 30-day process above, we may immediately restrict the affected access for a serious security threat or unlawful use. We will give written notice and 2 hours to stop that conduct where doing so is safe and practicable. For other material breaches, either party may terminate if the breach remains unresolved 30 days after written notice.
