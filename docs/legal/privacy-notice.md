This notice explains how Shatya Info handles personal data when someone visits our website, registers a company, uses an owner account, pays a bill, or contacts support. When a pharma company enters employee, doctor, customer, or field records into its workspace, that company determines why those records are used. We host and process them on its behalf under our Data Processing Agreement. Requests about those company-controlled records should usually go first to the company that entered them.

## 1. Data we collect for our own purposes

- **Registration and account:** company name and address, owner name, email, mobile number, password credentials, email-verification status, and workspace identifiers.
- **Billing:** licence count, invoices, payment status, Razorpay order and payment identifiers, and billing communications. Checkout is handled by Razorpay; we do not ask for card details in our registration form.
- **Support:** contact details, messages, and information you choose to share while we help you.
- **Technical and security:** IP address, browser and device information, request and authentication logs, CAPTCHA or anti-abuse signals, and cookies needed for sign-in and service operation.

Please do not send patient records or sensitive medical information through the public support form.

## 2. Why we use it

We use this information to verify email addresses, create and operate workspaces, authenticate users, prevent abuse and security incidents, issue and reconcile invoices, answer support requests, meet legal obligations, and communicate important service changes. We may send trial and billing reminders to the registered owner. We will describe any optional marketing messages and choice to opt out separately before sending them. We do not sell personal data or use Customer workspace data to train our own general-purpose AI models without separate written permission. If a Customer enables optional AI briefings, recent visit dates, outcomes and remarks, plus open follow-up dates, overdue flags and interaction text, may be sent to its selected provider. Free text may contain personal data. The [DPA's AI schedule](/legal/data-processing-agreement) gives more detail.

We process personal data under applicable Indian law using consent or another permitted ground that applies to the specific purpose. Where consent is required, you may withdraw it by contacting us, subject to lawful retention and the effect on the service.

## 3. Who receives data

Authorised Shatya Info personnel and service providers may access only the information needed for their work. Providers include application hosting, configured email delivery, Cloudflare for DNS or CAPTCHA, and Razorpay for checkout. Optional cloud AI is used only when a Customer enables it. We may disclose information when legally required or to protect users and the service. See the [DPA service-provider schedule](/legal/data-processing-agreement). We do not publish a customer's workspace records to other customers.

## 4. Where data is held and how long

The core application and database are hosted with DigitalOcean in India. Optional cloud AI, Cloudflare, Razorpay, and email providers may process information outside India. We keep registration, billing, support, and security records for as long as needed for the stated purposes and legal obligations. Customer workspace data is retained during the service. After service ends, the Customer has 30 days to request a support-assisted CSV or Excel export. We delete active copies within 7 days after a documented deletion request or the end of that window and backup copies within a further 15 days, unless law requires retention.

## 5. Security

We use individual accounts, role-based access, separate company databases, hashed passwords, and encryption for selected stored credentials. We also use operational safeguards for the hosted service. No online system can be guaranteed risk-free. We handle incidents and notifications under applicable law and our customer agreements.

## 6. Your choices and requests

Subject to applicable law, you may ask about personal data we control, request correction or erasure, withdraw consent where processing depends on consent, and raise a grievance. Contact **support@shatyainfo.com**. We may need to verify your identity. For records entered by your employer or a pharma company into its workspace, contact that company first; we will assist it in responding. You may also contact the applicable authority when the law provides that route.

## 7. Children and regulated information

The service is intended for business users, not children. Customers should not upload patient records, adverse-event case files, or GMP-critical records unless a separate written arrangement covers the necessary controls and responsibilities.

## 8. Changes

We will post an updated notice with its effective date and give additional notice of material changes where required. Earlier versions will be available on request at **support@shatyainfo.com**.
